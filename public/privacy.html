Privacy Policy

Last updated 20 August 2026

This is a draft and has not been reviewed by a lawyer. It describes accurately what the app does today, and it is written to be checked against the code rather than taken on trust. It is not legal advice, and it should be reviewed by a qualified lawyer before ATHLY IQ is offered to the public — particularly because ATHLY IQ is built for people under 18.

ATHLY IQ plans meals for teenage athletes. Doing that means holding information about a young person's body and what they eat, which is about as personal as data gets. This page says exactly what is stored, who else can see it, and how to get rid of it.

Who ATHLY IQ is for

ATHLY IQ is intended for athletes aged 13 and over. It is not directed to children under 13, and accounts should not be created for them. If we learn that an account belongs to someone under 13, we will delete it and everything attached to it.

If you are under 18, talk to a parent or guardian before you sign up. They can ask us what we hold about you, and they can ask us to delete it.

What ATHLY IQ stores

All of it comes from answers you give the app or from what you log in it. Nothing is inferred from anywhere else, and nothing is bought from anyone.

What Why it exists
Your first name, age, sex, height and weight Calorie and protein targets are calculated from them. See our published formulas.
Your goal, goal weight and pace The targets are calculated from your goal weight rather than your current one.
Your sports and training schedule, including session and lift times Meals are placed around when you train.
Foods you like, foods you dislike, and your allergies Allergies are a hard filter on everything the app suggests.
Meals you log — the name, the portion, and its calories, macronutrients and micronutrients Your daily rings, your history and your progress charts.
Weight, water and sleep you record The charts on the Progress screen.
Meal swaps and replanned days So your plan still looks like your plan when you come back to it.
Which agreements you accepted, and when So there is a record that you agreed to this policy and to AI reading your meal descriptions, and which version of each you saw.
Your email address Signing in. If you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive your email address from them.

What ATHLY IQ does not do

  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers. There are no ads in ATHLY IQ and no ad networks in it.
  • No analytics or tracking. There is no analytics SDK, no session recording, no third-party tracker and no cookie used for tracking.
  • Your data is never sold, and never shared for anyone else's marketing.
  • No photographs. ATHLY IQ does not take, upload or store pictures of you or your food.
  • No location. ATHLY IQ never asks for it and never records it.
  • No contacts, microphone or health-app data.

The camera

ATHLY IQ can scan a barcode on a food package. The camera is used only while that scanner is open, the image is never stored and never leaves your phone, and only the barcode number is read from it. You can refuse camera access and every other part of the app still works.

Who else is involved

Four services, each doing one job. None of them is paid to profile you, and none receives your data for their own purposes.

Service What it gets
Supabase — the database and sign-in Everything in the table above. This is where your account actually lives.
Vercel — hosting for the website Ordinary web-server information: your IP address and browser, as part of serving the page.
Google Fonts — the typeface the app is set in Your IP address, because the font is loaded from Google's servers when the app opens. No account information is sent, and Google is not told who you are. We intend to serve the font ourselves so this request stops happening.
Open Food Facts — the barcode database The barcode number you scanned, and nothing else. They are not told who scanned it.
Google or Apple — only if you choose to sign in with them They tell us your email address. We tell them nothing about you.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect someone from serious harm.

Where it is kept, and who can reach it

  • Your data is stored in Supabase, on servers in the United States.
  • It travels encrypted, and it is encrypted at rest.
  • Every table is protected by database-level rules that permit an account to read and write only its own rows. That is enforced by the database rather than by the app, so a bug in the app cannot expose one athlete's data to another. We test this on every change.
  • Deleting an account is done by a server-side function that identifies you from your verified sign-in — never from anything the app sends it.

How long it is kept

For as long as you have an account. When you delete your account, everything listed above is deleted with it, immediately and permanently. We keep one record of the deletion itself — an anonymous identifier and the date — so we can show that the request was carried out. It contains no name, no email address and nothing about your body or your food.

Your choices

  • See it. Everything ATHLY IQ holds about you is shown to you inside the app.
  • Correct it. Every answer can be changed on the Profile screen, and any logged meal can be removed.
  • Delete it. Profile → delete your account. It takes a few taps and there is no email to send.
  • Turn off reminders. Profile → Reminders, or in your phone's own settings.
  • Ask us. Write to us and we will answer, whoever you are and wherever you live.

Depending on where you live you may have additional rights — to a copy of your data, to have it corrected or erased, or to object to how it is used. Ask, and we will honour them.

Parents and guardians

If your child uses ATHLY IQ, you can ask us what we hold about them, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it and their account. Write to us from any address and tell us the email address on the account.

Changes to this policy

If this changes in a way that matters, we will say so in the app before the change takes effect rather than quietly updating this page. The date at the top always reflects the current version.
